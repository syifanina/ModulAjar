LET'S BUILD THE BASICS

A place for every digit.

Pahami caranya, lalu coba sendiri.

5 bite-sized lessons

✓
Make checking a habit.

Use the inverse operation to CHECK: undo addition with subtraction, and undo subtraction with addition.

Periksa jawaban menggunakan operasi kebalikan.

Start at the right: 5 is in the ones column. Its value is 5.

Mulai dari kanan: angka 5 berada di kolom satuan, nilainya 5.

8 is in the tens column: 8 tens have a value of 80.

Angka 8 di kolom puluhan bernilai 80.

7 is in the hundreds column. Its value is 700.

Angka 7 di kolom ratusan bernilai 700.

6 is in the thousands column. Its value is 6,000.

Angka 6 di kolom ribuan bernilai 6.000.

4 is in the ten-thousands column. Its value is 40,000. Moving one column left makes a place worth ten times as much.

Angka 4 bernilai 40.000. Setiap tempat di sebelah kiri bernilai sepuluh kali lipat.

Set out the numbers in columns. CARRYING is REGROUPING: exchange 10 of one unit for 1 of the next unit.

Menyimpan adalah pengelompokan ulang: tukar 10 satuan menjadi 1 puluhan.

Ones: 5 plus 7 makes 12. Write 2 ones and carry 1 ten above the tens column.

Satuan: 5 ditambah 7 menjadi 12. Tulis 2, simpan 1 ke kolom puluhan.

Tens: 8 plus 4 plus the carried 1 makes 13. Write 3 and carry 1 hundred.

Puluhan: 8 ditambah 4 ditambah simpanan 1 menjadi 13. Tulis 3, simpan 1 ratusan.

Hundreds: 7 plus 6 plus 1 makes 14. Write 4 and carry 1 thousand.

Ratusan: 7 ditambah 6 ditambah 1 menjadi 14. Tulis 4, simpan 1 ribuan.

Thousands: 6 plus 8 plus 1 makes 15. Write 5 and carry 1 ten-thousand.

Ribuan: 6 ditambah 8 ditambah 1 menjadi 15. Tulis 5, simpan 1 puluh ribuan.

Ten-thousands: 4 plus 2 plus 1 makes 7. The total is 75,432.

Puluh ribuan: 4 ditambah 2 ditambah 1 menjadi 7. Hasilnya 75.432.

2 ones cannot subtract 6 ones without BORROWING. We call this EXCHANGING. The tens and hundreds are both zero, so look to the thousands.

2 satuan tidak cukup untuk dikurangi 6. Puluhan dan ratusan nol, jadi mulai menukar dari ribuan.

Exchange 1 thousand for 10 hundreds. There are now 4 thousands and 10 hundreds.

Tukar 1 ribuan menjadi 10 ratusan. Tersisa 4 ribuan.

Exchange 1 of those hundreds for 10 tens. Now there are 9 hundreds and 10 tens.

Tukar 1 ratusan menjadi 10 puluhan. Tersisa 9 ratusan.

Exchange 1 ten for 10 ones. Now there are 9 tens and 12 ones. Subtract 6 ones from 12 to write 6.

Tukar 1 puluhan menjadi 10 satuan. Kini ada 9 puluhan dan 12 satuan. Kurangi 6 satuan, hasilnya 6.

Tens: 9 take away 8 gives 1. Hundreds: 9 take away 7 gives 2. Thousands: 4 take away 2 gives 2.

Puluhan: 9 dikurangi 8 menjadi 1. Ratusan: 9 dikurangi 7 menjadi 2. Ribuan: 4 dikurangi 2 menjadi 2.

CHECK with addition. The difference plus the amount taken away gives the starting number, 5,002.

Periksa dengan penjumlahan. Selisih ditambah bilangan pengurang menghasilkan bilangan awal, 5.002.

Align the DECIMAL POINT. Add a zero so 12.7 becomes 12.70; the value stays the same.

Sejajarkan titik desimal. Tambahkan nol agar 12.7 menjadi 12.70; nilainya tetap.

Hundredths: 0 plus 5 gives 5. Tenths: 7 plus 8 gives 15. Write 5 tenths and carry 1 to the ones column.

Perseratusan: hasilnya 5. Persepuluhan: 7 ditambah 8 menjadi 15. Tulis 5 dan simpan 1 satuan.

Ones: 2 plus 3 plus 1 gives 6. Tens: 1 remains. Bring the decimal point straight down.

Satuan: 2 ditambah 3 ditambah 1 menjadi 6. Puluhan tetap 1. Turunkan titik desimal.

For decimal subtraction, align the points again. Exchange across the zeros, just as with whole numbers.

Untuk pengurangan desimal, sejajarkan titik dan lakukan penukaran melewati nol.

Exchange 1 ten into 10 ones, then 1 one into 10 tenths, then 1 tenth into 10 hundredths. Subtract right to left: 10 take away 6 gives 4; 9 take away 7 gives 2; 9 take away 4 gives 5. The answer is 5.24.

Tukar bertahap hingga tersisa 9 satuan, 9 persepuluhan, dan 10 perseratusan. Kurangi dari kanan. Hasilnya 5.24.

Choose the operations. “Receives more” means add; “lends out” means subtract. First find the total after receiving books.

Tentukan operasi: menerima berarti tambah, meminjamkan berarti kurang. Hitung jumlah setelah menerima buku.

After adding the new books, the library has 1,625 books.

Setelah penjumlahan, perpustakaan memiliki 1.625 buku.

Subtract the 248 books lent out. There are 1,377 books remaining. Include the unit in your conclusion.

Kurangi 248 buku yang dipinjamkan. Tersisa 1.377 buku. Sertakan satuan pada kesimpulan.

CHECK: adding back the borrowed books gives 1,625, the total before lending. The answer makes sense.

Periksa: tambahkan kembali buku yang dipinjam. Hasilnya 1.625, jumlah sebelum dipinjamkan.
